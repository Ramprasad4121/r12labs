# Design System — R12 Labs

## Product Context

- **What this is:** The public site for a formal-verification and blockchain-security firm.
- **Who it is for:** Protocol founders, engineering leads, security teams, technical partners, and investors evaluating a specialist firm.
- **Project type:** Institutional marketing site with a direct project-inquiry flow.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Direction:** Editorial assurance.
- **Decoration level:** Minimal. Credibility comes from typography, restraint, and precise language instead of visual effects.
- **Mood:** A careful research firm on paper, with one controlled technical surface for proof artifacts.
- **Research basis:** Certora, Runtime Verification, Trail of Bits, and OpenZeppelin establish that clear scope, explicit methodology, public evidence, and non-overstated guarantees are the category baseline. R12 differentiates through quieter presentation and a proof-boundary-first message.

## Typography

- **Display:** Source Serif 4 for headlines and section titles. It brings measured authority without feeling like a fintech template.
- **Body and UI:** IBM Plex Sans for readable engineering and business copy.
- **Technical labels:** IBM Plex Mono only where code, a property, or a compact status label benefits from it.
- **Scale:** Body 17px; section titles 36–48px; home display 48–72px, responsive.

## Color

- **Paper:** `#f7f5f0`.
- **Ink:** `#18232c`.
- **Muted ink:** `#65707a`.
- **Rule:** `#d9d5cc`.
- **Signal:** `#1f4f6b`, reserved for meaningful actions, links, and small labels.
- **Panel:** `#efede7`.
- **Inverse:** `#fffdf8` on ink fields.

## Layout

- **Approach:** Editorial, grid-disciplined, and intentionally sparse.
- **Max width:** 1152px.
- **Spacing:** Comfortable 4px-based scale with 56–96px section rhythm on desktop.
- **Surfaces:** Flat planes, hairline rules, square edges. Cards are used only when they carry a distinct task.

## Motion

- **Approach:** Minimal-functional.
- **Durations:** 150ms for link, button, and icon transitions.
- **Rule:** Never animate layout or use decorative motion to simulate complexity.

## Writing Rules

- Do not add numbered section labels unless a sequence genuinely requires them.
- Do not call a result a proof without naming its property, model, and assumptions.
- Prefer one precise sentence over three promotional ones.
- Do not invent client names, protected value, credentials, or case-study metrics.
